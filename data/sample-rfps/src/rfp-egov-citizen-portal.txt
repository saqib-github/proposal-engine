1. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

1.1 Purpose of This RFP

The Provincial Information Technology Authority (hereinafter "PITA" or "the Authority") invites sealed proposals from eligible, experienced and financially sound information technology firms for the design, development, implementation and three (3) year operation and maintenance of the Provincial e-Services Citizen Portal (hereinafter "the Portal" or "the System"). This Request for Proposals ("RFP") sets out the background and objectives of the assignment, the scope of work, the mandatory eligibility criteria, the technical and functional requirements, the evaluation criteria and methodology, the submission instructions, and the principal commercial terms that will govern the resulting contract.

1.2 About the Authority

PITA is the statutory body responsible for the digital transformation of provincial government departments. The Authority operates the provincial government data centre, the government wide area network, and the official online service delivery channels of the Provincial Government. PITA reports to the Provincial Cabinet Committee on Digitisation and is mandated under the Digital Province Strategy 2025-2030 to consolidate citizen-facing services onto a single, secure, bilingual digital platform accessible from web and mobile channels.

1.3 Project Rationale

At present, citizens of the Province must visit multiple departmental offices in person to obtain certificates, licences, permits and registrations. Service delivery remains largely paper-based, turnaround times are long and unpredictable, and there is no unified mechanism for online payment of government fees or for tracking the status of an application. Departmental record rooms hold decades of paper files, and the few departmental web systems that do exist operate in isolation, with inconsistent user experiences and no single citizen identity. The Provincial Government has therefore approved the e-Services Citizen Portal Programme to deliver at least forty (40) high-volume public services through a single online platform within eighteen (18) months of contract award, followed by a three-year managed operation period.

1.4 Funding

The Programme is funded through the Provincial Annual Development Programme (ADP Scheme No. DP-2026-0417). Funds covering the full contract term, including the three-year operation and maintenance period, stand allocated and released in accordance with the approved PC-I. No part of this procurement is contingent on donor financing.

3. SCOPE OF WORK

3.1 Overview

The successful Bidder shall design, build, test, deploy, operate and maintain the Provincial e-Services Citizen Portal as a turnkey solution. The scope of work comprises eight workstreams: (a) design and development of the citizen portal and administrative back office; (b) digitisation and workflow automation of at least forty (40) citizen e-services; (c) integration with payment gateways and core government systems; (d) native mobile applications for Android and iOS; (e) migration of data from legacy departmental systems; (f) deployment on in-country infrastructure; (g) establishment and operation of a citizen facilitation helpdesk; and (h) training, change management and knowledge transfer. The Bidder shall be responsible for end-to-end delivery, including project management, quality assurance, information security compliance and warranty support, for the duration of the Contract.

3.2 Citizen Portal and User Experience

The Portal shall provide a single point of access for citizens, businesses and government users. It shall offer account registration and login using CNIC-based identity verification, a personalised citizen dashboard, application submission with document upload, real-time application status tracking, electronic payment of fees and taxes, downloadable digitally signed certificates, and a grievance redressal module. The entire public-facing experience shall be fully bilingual in Urdu and English, including right-to-left rendering for Urdu content, and shall conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility requirements. The Portal must be responsive across desktop, tablet and smartphone form factors and must function correctly on low-bandwidth connections of 256 kbps or better.

3.3 e-Services Catalogue

The Bidder shall digitise, configure and operate no fewer than forty (40) e-services in consultation with the participating departments. The indicative service catalogue includes, at a minimum:

- Issuance and renewal of domicile certificates;
- Birth, death, marriage and divorce registration and certificate issuance;
- Issuance of character certificates and police verification requests;
- Registration of sole proprietorships and partnership firms;
- Trade and professional licence applications and renewals;
- Vehicle route permits and fitness certificate bookings;
- Land record fard issuance and mutation status tracking;
- Payment of property tax, professional tax and motor vehicle tax;
- Pension status enquiry and pensioner life certification;
- Scholarship applications and verification of educational documents;
- Appointment booking for provincial service centres;
- Right-to-information requests and citizen feedback submissions.

Each e-service shall be implemented with a configurable workflow engine supporting multi-step approvals, delegations, escalations, automatic SMS and email notifications at every state change, and service-level timers aligned with the Provincial Citizens Charter.

3.4 Payment Gateway Integration

The System shall integrate with at least two licensed payment aggregators operating in Pakistan and shall support payment through debit and credit cards, internet and mobile banking (including 1LINK and RAAST rails), over-the-counter bank challans with barcode, and mobile wallets. The System shall generate PSID-based challans reconcilable with the provincial treasury, shall perform automated daily reconciliation, and shall provide a refunds and chargeback handling workflow. All payment flows must be PCI DSS compliant to the extent applicable to the integration model adopted.

3.5 Mobile Applications

The Bidder shall deliver native or cross-platform mobile applications for Android (minimum Android 9) and iOS (minimum iOS 14), published under the Authority's developer accounts. The mobile applications shall provide functional parity with the Portal for at least the twenty (20) highest-volume services, shall support biometric login, push notifications, offline draft saving, and in-app payment, and shall be fully bilingual in Urdu and English.

3.6 Data Migration from Legacy Systems

The Bidder shall plan and execute the migration of records from the legacy departmental systems described in Annex D, which include: (a) the District Records Management System operating on Oracle 11g with approximately 9.2 million records; (b) the Licence Management System operating on SQL Server 2014 with approximately 1.4 million records; and (c) departmental Microsoft Access and Excel registers estimated at 600,000 rows. The Bidder shall be responsible for data profiling, cleansing rules, de-duplication, transformation, migration rehearsal runs, reconciliation reports with record-level audit trails, and sign-off of migration completeness with each data-owning department. Legacy systems shall remain operational in read-only mode until migration sign-off.

3.7 Hosting and Infrastructure

The System shall be deployed within the territory of Pakistan. The primary site shall be the PITA Government Data Centre, with a disaster recovery site at a geographically separate in-country facility providing a recovery point objective (RPO) of no more than 15 minutes and a recovery time objective (RTO) of no more than 4 hours. Citizen data shall not be stored, processed or replicated outside Pakistan under any circumstances. The Bidder shall size, install and configure the runtime environment using the Authority-provided virtualised infrastructure, and shall implement environment separation for development, testing, staging and production.

3.8 Helpdesk and Service Operations

The Bidder shall establish and operate a citizen facilitation helpdesk providing support in Urdu and English from 08:00 to 22:00 PKT, seven days a week, through telephone, email, web chat and an in-portal ticketing system. The Bidder shall also provide second-line and third-line technical support for the System on a 24x7 basis for Severity 1 incidents. The System shall achieve a monthly service availability of at least 99.9 percent, measured at the production application tier and excluding agreed maintenance windows, throughout the O&M Period.

3.9 Training and Capacity Building

The Bidder shall design and deliver a structured training programme covering: end-user training for at least 800 departmental officers across the Province; administrator and operations training for up to 40 PITA engineers; train-the-trainer sessions for 60 master trainers; and executive briefings for departmental leadership. Training shall be delivered in a blended mode with classroom sessions in the provincial capital and divisional headquarters, e-learning modules hosted on the Portal, bilingual user manuals, and quick reference guides. A comprehensive knowledge transfer and exit management plan shall be executed during the final six months of the O&M Period.

3.10 Implementation Phases

The assignment shall be delivered in the following phases:

Phase | Key Deliverables | Completion (from Contract Signing)
Phase 1 - Inception and Design | Inception report, detailed system requirements specification, solution architecture document, UX prototypes | Month 3
Phase 2 - Core Platform Build | Portal core, workflow engine, citizen identity, payment integration, first 10 e-services in staging | Month 8
Phase 3 - Pilot Go-Live | Pilot launch of 10 e-services in 3 districts, mobile applications beta, data migration wave 1 | Month 12
Phase 4 - Full Rollout | All 40+ e-services live province-wide, data migration completed, full helpdesk operational | Month 18
Phase 5 - Operation and Maintenance | 36-month O&M period, SLA reporting, continuous improvement, exit management | Month 18 to Month 54

6. TECHNICAL AND FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

6.1 Architecture and Technology

- TR-01: The System shall be designed on a modular, service-oriented or microservices architecture with documented APIs for every service module.
- TR-02: The System shall expose all integration interfaces as versioned RESTful APIs secured with OAuth 2.0, and shall publish an internal API catalogue with machine-readable specifications.
- TR-03: The System shall be deployable on the Authority's virtualised on-premises infrastructure without dependence on any proprietary public cloud service.
- TR-04: The System shall support horizontal scaling to no fewer than 2,000 concurrent authenticated user sessions and 25,000 daily applications at full rollout, with a page response time not exceeding 3 seconds at the 95th percentile under peak load.
- TR-05: The System shall use open standards for all stored documents and shall digitally sign issued certificates using PKI certificates from a licensed certification authority in Pakistan.

6.2 Portal Functionality

- TR-06: The Portal shall provide bilingual content management in Urdu and English, with all citizen-facing screens, notifications, receipts and certificates rendered in the language selected by the user.
- TR-07: The Portal shall conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, verified through an independent accessibility audit prior to Go-Live.
- TR-08: The Portal shall provide a configurable forms engine permitting Authority administrators to create and modify service application forms, checklists and fee rules without vendor code changes.
- TR-09: The Portal shall provide a citizen document wallet in which verified certificates and previously uploaded documents are reusable across services.
- TR-10: The Portal shall provide dashboards and operational reports for each department, including service volumes, processing times, SLA breaches and revenue collected, with export to Excel and CSV.

6.3 Identity, Payments and Integrations

- TR-11: The System shall verify citizen identity against the national identity database through the Authority's existing verification gateway, and shall log every verification transaction.
- TR-12: The System shall integrate with at least two licensed payment aggregators and shall support card, account-to-account, wallet and over-the-counter challan payments with automated end-of-day reconciliation.
- TR-13: The System shall provide SMS and email notification services through the Authority's empanelled gateways, with delivery status tracking and configurable bilingual templates.
- TR-14: The System shall integrate with the provincial land records, civil registration and treasury systems through the interface specifications provided in Annex E.

6.4 Data Migration

- TR-15: The Bidder shall submit a detailed data migration strategy, including profiling results, cleansing and transformation rules, rehearsal plans and rollback procedures, for approval prior to the first migration wave.
- TR-16: Each migration wave shall conclude with a reconciliation report demonstrating 100 percent record accountability, with discrepancies itemised and dispositioned in consultation with the data-owning department.

6.5 Security and Privacy

- TR-17: The System shall implement role-based access control with segregation of duties, multi-factor authentication for all administrative accounts, and a tamper-evident audit trail of all create, update, delete and view operations on citizen records.
- TR-18: The System shall encrypt all data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and all sensitive data at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent approved algorithm.
- TR-19: The Bidder shall arrange an independent vulnerability assessment and penetration test by a firm approved by the Authority before Go-Live and annually thereafter, and shall remediate all critical and high findings before public launch.
- TR-20: The System and all associated operational processes shall comply with the Authority's Information Security Policy and applicable data protection legislation of Pakistan; citizen data shall be hosted exclusively within Pakistan.
- TR-21: The Bidder shall appoint a dedicated, certified information security officer for the duration of the Contract and shall report security incidents to the Authority within four (4) hours of detection.

6.6 Service Management and Support

- TR-22: The Bidder shall operate the helpdesk and technical support functions in accordance with ITIL-aligned incident, problem, change and release management processes, supported by a service management tool with ticket-level reporting.
- TR-23: The System shall be monitored through an application performance monitoring toolset with automated alerting, and monthly SLA reports shall be submitted to the Authority within five (5) working days of month end.
- TR-24: The Bidder shall maintain the System at vendor-supported versions of all third-party components throughout the O&M Period, applying security patches within the timelines defined in the SLA.

6.7 Training and Documentation

- TR-25: The Bidder shall deliver the training programme described in Section 3.9 and shall achieve a post-training assessment pass rate of at least 80 percent among trained officers.
- TR-26: The Bidder shall deliver and maintain complete documentation, including system design, API specifications, operations runbooks, administrator manuals and bilingual end-user guides, updated with every major release.

7. SERVICE LEVELS AND OPERATIONS

7.1 Service Availability

The System shall achieve a monthly availability of at least 99.9 percent during the O&M Period, measured at the production application tier, twenty-four hours a day, excluding scheduled maintenance windows agreed at least five (5) working days in advance and limited to a maximum of four (4) hours per calendar month between 00:00 and 06:00 PKT.

7.2 Incident Response and Resolution

Incidents shall be classified and serviced according to the following matrix:

Severity | Definition | Response Time | Resolution Time
Severity 1 | Portal or payment services unavailable to all users | 15 minutes | 4 hours
Severity 2 | Major function degraded; no workaround available | 30 minutes | 8 hours
Severity 3 | Minor function impaired; workaround available | 2 hours | 3 working days
Severity 4 | Cosmetic issue or service request | 8 hours | 10 working days

7.3 Service Credits

Failure to meet the availability target or the resolution times in Section 7.2 shall trigger the service credits set out in the SLA, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%) of the quarterly operations fee for the affected quarter. Service credits are in addition to, and not in substitution of, the Authority's other contractual remedies.

8. EVALUATION CRITERIA AND METHODOLOGY

8.1 Two-Envelope Evaluation

Proposals shall be evaluated under the Single Stage - Two Envelope procedure. Technical envelopes shall be opened first and evaluated against the criteria in Section 8.2. Financial envelopes of only those Bidders whose technical proposals achieve the minimum qualifying technical score shall be opened publicly at a date and time notified in advance; financial envelopes of technically non-responsive Bidders shall be returned unopened.

8.2 Evaluation Criteria and Weights

The total evaluation score is 100 points, distributed as follows:

Evaluation Criterion | Weight (Points)
Technical Approach and Solution Design | 25
Relevant Experience and Past Performance | 20
Key Personnel and Team Composition | 15
Project Management and Delivery Plan | 10
Information Security and Compliance | 10
Financial Proposal | 20
Total | 100

8.3 Minimum Technical Qualifying Score

The technical evaluation shall be scored out of the eighty (80) points allocated to the five technical criteria. A Bidder must score at least fifty-six (56) of the 80 technical points, that is seventy percent (70%), to be declared technically qualified. The responses to the Technical Questionnaire in Section 11 shall be scored within the Technical Approach and Solution Design criterion.

8.4 Financial Scoring

The lowest evaluated financial proposal among technically qualified Bidders shall receive the full 20 financial points. Each other financial proposal shall receive points calculated as: Financial Score = 20 multiplied by (Lowest Evaluated Price divided by Evaluated Price of the Proposal under consideration). The Contract shall be awarded to the Bidder achieving the highest combined technical and financial score.
