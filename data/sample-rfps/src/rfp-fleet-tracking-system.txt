1. INTRODUCTION

Karachi Metropolitan Distribution Services (hereinafter "KMDS" or "the Company") operates a distribution fleet of approximately 450 vehicles, comprising 280 delivery vans, 120 rigid trucks and 50 refrigerated units, serving retail and wholesale customers across Karachi and the wider region from three distribution centres. Fleet operations are currently managed through manual trip sheets, standalone fuel records and a basic vehicle log register, resulting in poor route discipline, unverified fuel consumption, late deliveries and limited visibility of vehicle utilisation. KMDS invites quotations from qualified information technology and telematics firms for the supply, implementation and support of an integrated Fleet Tracking and Logistics Management Platform (hereinafter "the Platform") covering GPS/IoT vehicle tracking, route planning and optimisation, driver behaviour scoring, fuel monitoring, warehouse dispatch integration, management dashboards and a two-year support and maintenance period. This Request for Quotations ("RFQ") describes the scope of supply and services, eligibility criteria, technical requirements, evaluation methodology and submission instructions.

2. SCOPE OF SUPPLY AND SERVICES

2.1 Summary of Scope

The successful bidder (hereinafter "the Supplier") shall be responsible for:

- Supply, installation and commissioning of GPS/IoT tracking devices in 450 fleet vehicles, plus five percent (5%) spare units held in stock for swap-out;
- Supply and installation of fuel level sensors in the 170 trucks and refrigerated units, calibrated per vehicle tank profile;
- Supply and installation of temperature sensors in the 50 refrigerated units with in-cab and platform alerts;
- Implementation and configuration of the central fleet tracking and logistics management software, hosted in Pakistan;
- Integration of the Platform with the existing KMDS warehouse management system for dispatch and proof-of-delivery data exchange;
- A driver mobile application supporting job assignment, navigation, electronic proof of delivery and panic alerts;
- Migration of vehicle master data, driver records and customer delivery locations from existing registers and spreadsheets;
- Training of dispatchers, fleet supervisors, workshop staff and management users;
- Two (2) years of comprehensive support, maintenance, device warranty and airtime/connectivity management from the date of operational acceptance.

5. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

5.1 Tracking Devices and Sensors

- FT-01: The Supplier shall provide GPS/IoT tracking devices supporting GPS and GLONASS positioning, 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback, an internal backup battery of at least 4 hours, ignition sensing, and a minimum ingress protection rating of IP65 for externally mounted components.
- FT-02: Devices shall report position, speed, heading, ignition state and configured event data at intervals not exceeding 10 seconds while in motion and 5 minutes while stationary, with store-and-forward buffering of at least 5,000 records during connectivity loss.
- FT-03: Fuel level sensors shall achieve an accuracy of plus or minus 3 percent after per-vehicle calibration, and the Platform shall detect and alert refuelling and fuel drain events with timestamp, location and estimated volume.
- FT-04: Temperature sensors in refrigerated units shall report at intervals not exceeding 60 seconds, with configurable thresholds and breach alerts to dispatchers and in the driver application.
- FT-05: The Supplier shall manage SIM provisioning and airtime for all devices for the full two-year support period, with monthly connectivity reporting.

5.2 Platform Functionality

- FT-06: The Platform shall provide live map tracking of all 450 vehicles with at most 15 seconds of latency, historical trip playback for a minimum of 12 months online, and geofencing with entry, exit and dwell-time alerts.
- FT-07: The Platform shall provide route planning and optimisation that produces multi-stop delivery routes for at least 300 daily orders across the three distribution centres, accounting for vehicle capacity, delivery time windows and zone restrictions, and shall recalculate routes when orders change.
- FT-08: The Platform shall compute a driver behaviour score per driver per period, based at a minimum on harsh braking, harsh acceleration, over-speeding against configurable road-class limits, sharp cornering, excessive idling and unauthorised stops, and shall produce ranked driver league reports.
- FT-09: The Platform shall integrate with the KMDS warehouse management system through documented REST APIs to receive dispatch orders and return delivery status and electronic proof-of-delivery records, including recipient name, signature image, photo and geo-stamp.
- FT-10: The Platform shall provide role-based dashboards for dispatchers, fleet supervisors and management, including fleet utilisation, on-time delivery percentage, kilometres run, fuel consumption versus benchmark, maintenance due lists and exception summaries, with scheduled email reports and export to Excel and CSV.
- FT-11: The Platform shall maintain a vehicle maintenance module covering service schedules by kilometres and calendar, defect reporting from the driver application, and workshop job records.
- FT-12: The driver mobile application shall operate on Android 10 or later, shall support Urdu and English interfaces, and shall function in offline mode with automatic synchronisation upon reconnection.
- FT-13: The Platform shall be hosted in a data centre or cloud region located in Pakistan, shall enforce role-based access control with multi-factor authentication for administrative users, shall encrypt data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and shall provide a complete audit trail of user actions.
- FT-14: The Platform shall provide open, documented APIs sufficient for KMDS to extract its own data, and all tracking and operational data shall remain the property of KMDS and shall be handed over in a standard machine-readable format upon contract end.

5.3 Data Migration and Training

- FT-15: The Supplier shall migrate vehicle master data, driver records and approximately 6,500 customer delivery locations from existing spreadsheets and registers, with validation and sign-off by KMDS operations.
- FT-16: The Supplier shall deliver role-based training for up to 12 dispatchers, 10 fleet supervisors, 8 workshop staff and 6 managers, together with bilingual quick reference guides and administrator documentation.

6. SUPPORT, WARRANTY AND SERVICE LEVELS

The Supplier shall provide comprehensive support and maintenance for twenty-four (24) months from operational acceptance, including all software updates, device warranty with advance replacement from the spares pool, sensor recalibration, airtime management and helpdesk support from 08:00 to 20:00 PKT, seven days a week. The Platform shall achieve a monthly availability of at least 99.5 percent, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least three (3) working days in advance. Critical faults (Platform unavailable or live tracking down fleet-wide) shall be responded to within 30 minutes and resolved within 6 hours; faulty in-vehicle devices shall be replaced within 2 working days in Karachi. The Supplier shall provide a quarterly service review report covering availability, fault statistics, device health and connectivity performance. Persistent failure to meet the availability target for three (3) consecutive months shall entitle KMDS to terminate the contract and recover the unutilised portion of the support fees.

9. TECHNICAL QUESTIONNAIRE

Bidders shall answer the following questions in the technical envelope. Each answer shall not exceed two (2) pages and shall be specific to KMDS operations:

Q1. Device Specification and Certification: Specify the make and model of the proposed tracking devices, fuel sensors and temperature sensors, their PTA type approval status, mounting and tamper protection approach, and your device failure rate experience from comparable deployments.

Q2. Platform Architecture: Describe the Platform architecture, including the hosting arrangement in Pakistan, multi-tenancy model if any, data ingestion capacity, latency safeguards for live tracking of 450 vehicles, backup and disaster recovery arrangements, and the security controls applied at each layer.

Q3. Route Optimisation Approach: Explain how your route optimisation engine builds and re-optimises multi-stop routes under vehicle capacity, time window and zone constraints, the typical computation time for a 300-order daily plan, and how dispatchers can manually adjust optimised routes.

Q4. WMS Integration: Describe your proposed approach to integrating with the KMDS warehouse management system, including the API patterns, message formats, error handling and reconciliation arrangements, and a summary of two comparable integrations you have delivered.

Q5. Driver Scoring Methodology: Describe the events, weightings and normalisation method used in your driver behaviour scoring, how scores remain fair across different routes and vehicle types, and how the scoring has measurably reduced incidents or fuel consumption in past deployments.

Q6. Support and Spares Model: Describe your support organisation for this contract, including Karachi-based resources, escalation tiers, the spares pool and advance replacement process, sensor recalibration schedule, and the reporting you will provide against the service levels in Section 6.

11. PENALTIES AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

If the Supplier fails to achieve operational acceptance (milestone M5) within twenty (20) weeks of purchase order for reasons attributable to the Supplier, KMDS shall deduct liquidated damages at the rate of 0.25 percent (zero point two five percent) of the total contract price per week of delay or part thereof, up to a maximum of eight percent (8%) of the total contract price, after which KMDS may cancel the purchase order, encash the bid security or performance security then held, and procure the platform from another supplier at the Supplier's risk and cost. During the support period, service credits of 2 percent of the quarterly support fee shall apply for each 0.1 percentage point shortfall below the 99.5 percent monthly availability target, capped at 20 percent of the quarterly support fee. The successful bidder shall furnish a performance security of five percent (5%) of the contract value within ten (10) days of the purchase order, valid for the full contract term plus sixty (60) days.
