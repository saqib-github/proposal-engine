1. INTRODUCTION
National Food Logistics Company (Private) Limited (hereinafter "NFLC" or "the Employer") is a state-owned
logistics enterprise responsible for the procurement, storage and distribution of strategic food commodities.
NFLC invites sealed bids from eligible and pre-qualified construction firms for the construction of a Regional
Warehouse and Cold Storage Facility with a covered area of approximately 5,000 square metres, together
with associated external works, at the Southern Regional Logistics Park. The works comprise civil and
structural construction, insulated cold storage chambers, HVAC and industrial refrigeration plant, electrical
and mechanical services, fire detection and suppression, and site development. This tender document sets
out the scope of works, eligibility requirements, key dates, bill of quantities structure, evaluation criteria and
conditions of contract.
2. SCOPE OF WORKS
2.1 General Description
The contractor shall execute and complete the following principal components, in accordance with the
drawings, technical specifications and bill of quantities issued with this tender:
•   Earthworks, excavation and site grading for an overall plot of 2.5 acres, including dewatering where
required;
•   Reinforced concrete foundations, columns and grade slabs designed for a uniform floor loading of 5
tonnes per square metre in the dry warehouse zone;
•   Pre-engineered steel building (PEB) superstructure of approximately 5,000 sqm covered area with a clear
internal height of 10.5 metres at eaves;
•   Construction of four (4) insulated cold storage chambers totalling 1,800 sqm with PUF insulated panels
(150 mm walls and ceiling), operating at temperature ranges of +2 to +8 degrees Celsius (two chambers)
and -18 to -25 degrees Celsius (two chambers);
•   Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of the industrial refrigeration plant, including compressor
racks, evaporators, condensers, refrigerant piping, insulated doors, air curtains and a plant monitoring
system;
•   HVAC works for the administration block, battery charging room and quality control laboratory;
•   Electrical works including an 11 kV substation, 630 kVA transformer, LT panels, power and lighting
distribution, earthing, lightning protection and a 500 kVA standby diesel generator;
•   Fire detection and addressable alarm system, hydrant network, sprinkler protection in the dry warehouse,
and clean-agent suppression in the server and control rooms;
•   Loading docks (8 bays) with dock levellers, dock shelters and high-speed doors;
•   Internal roads, paved marshalling yard for 25 trucks, boundary wall, guard rooms, weighbridge foundation
(60-tonne), water supply, underground and overhead water tanks, and sewerage and storm water
drainage;
•   Administration block of 400 sqm covered area, including offices, records room, laboratory and staff
facilities.
2.2 Design Responsibility
The works shall be executed on a construct-only basis against the Employer's issued-for-construction
drawings, except for the refrigeration plant and the pre-engineered steel building, for which the contractor
shall provide detailed shop drawings and design calculations, prepared and stamped by a professional
engineer registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), for the Employer's approval prior to
fabrication.

2.3 Completion Period
The entire works shall be completed and handed over, tested and commissioned, within fourteen (14) months
from the date of the Engineer's notice to commence. A detailed construction programme in Primavera P6 or
Microsoft Project format shall be submitted within fourteen (14) days of the notice to commence and shall be
updated monthly.
2.4 Materials and Workmanship
All materials shall conform to the technical specifications and to the relevant Pakistan Standards or, where no
Pakistan Standard exists, to the applicable British or ASTM standards named in the specifications. Structural
concrete shall be batched at an approved plant with cube testing at a site laboratory; reinforcement steel shall
be Grade 60 deformed bars from an approved mill with mill test certificates for every consignment. PUF
insulated panels shall carry factory test certificates for density, thermal conductivity and fire classification. The
contractor shall submit material approval requests, with samples where required, at least fourteen (14) days
before intended use, and no material shall be incorporated in the works without the Engineer's written
approval.
3. MANDATORY ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Bidders must satisfy all of the following mandatory requirements. Non-compliance with any requirement shall
render the bid non-responsive:
•   EC-1:   The bidder must hold a valid constructor licence issued by the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC)
in category C-2 or above, with the licence valid for the year 2026 and covering the relevant specialisation
codes (CE-01 civil works and ME-05 HVACR). PEC licence category C-2 is mandatory; bids submitted by
firms licensed below category C-2 shall be rejected.
•   EC-2:   The bidder must be registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan or the
Registrar of Firms, and with the Federal Board of Revenue (NTN and STRN), and must appear on the
Active Taxpayers List.
•   EC-3:   The bidder must have completed, within the last eight (8) years, at least two (2) industrial or
warehouse construction projects each of contract value not less than PKR 250 million, of which at least
one must have included cold storage or industrial refrigeration works. Completion certificates from the
employers concerned must be enclosed.
•   EC-4:   The bidder must have an average annual construction turnover of not less than PKR 400 million
over the last three (3) audited financial years, supported by audited financial statements.
•   EC-5:   The bidder must demonstrate access to liquid assets or unconditional credit lines of not less than
PKR 80 million, supported by a bank certificate issued not earlier than thirty (30) days before the bid
submission deadline.
•   EC-6:   The bidder must submit an affidavit that it is not blacklisted or debarred by any federal, provincial or
local government department, autonomous body or public sector organisation in Pakistan.
•   EC-7:   The bidder must furnish bid security equal to three percent (3%) of the bid price in the form of a
bank guarantee or pay order from a scheduled bank of Pakistan.
•   EC-8:   The bidder shall deploy a full-time site agent who is a PEC-registered professional civil engineer
with at least ten (10) years of post-registration experience in industrial building construction, supported by
a PEC-registered mechanical engineer with at least seven (7) years of experience in refrigeration and
HVAC installations. CVs, PEC registration numbers and undertakings of availability must be submitted
with the bid.
•   EC-9:   The bidder must have a documented health, safety and environment (HSE) management system;
ISO 45001 certification or an equivalent third-party-audited HSE system is mandatory.

5.1 Estimated Cost
The Engineer's estimated cost of the works is PKR 480,000,000 (Pakistani Rupees four hundred and eighty

shall be evaluated against the bill of quantities rates quoted by the bidder. Bids exceeding the estimated cost

6. HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT REQUIREMENTS
The contractor shall implement a site-specific HSE plan, which must be submitted within fourteen (14) days of
the notice to commence and approved by the Engineer before mobilisation of major plant. The HSE plan shall
address, at a minimum: scaffolding and work at height; lifting operations and crane management; confined
space entry; ammonia handling and refrigerant safety during charging and commissioning; hot works and
welding; electrical safety and lock-out tag-out; dust and noise control; waste management; and emergency
response. The contractor shall deploy a full-time qualified HSE officer for the duration of the works, shall
conduct daily toolbox talks, and shall report all incidents, including near misses, within twenty-four (24) hours.
The Employer operates a zero-fatality policy; any fatal accident attributable to contractor negligence shall
constitute grounds for termination. All workers shall be provided with personal protective equipment and shall
be covered under workmen's compensation insurance. Monthly HSE statistics shall be appended to each
interim payment certificate.
7. EVALUATION CRITERIA
Bids shall first be screened against the mandatory eligibility criteria in Section 3. Responsive bids shall then
be scored against the following criteria; the contract shall be awarded to the highest-scoring responsive
bidder:
Evaluation Criterion   Weight (Points)
Relevant Construction Experience and Completion Record   25
Technical Staff, Plant and Equipment Capacity   15
Construction Methodology and Programme   15
Financial Soundness   10
Bid Price   35
Total   100
A minimum score of forty (40) points out of the sixty-five (65) non-price points is required for the bid price to
be evaluated. The lowest evaluated bid price shall receive the full 35 points; other bids shall receive points in
inverse proportion to their evaluated price.
8. TECHNICAL QUESTIONNAIRE
Bidders shall provide written answers to the following questions as part of their technical submission. Each
answer shall not exceed two (2) pages:
Q1.   Construction Methodology: Describe your overall construction methodology and sequencing for
completing the warehouse, cold storage chambers and external works within fourteen (14) months,
including temporary works, concurrent work fronts and your approach to maintaining programme float.
Q2.   Refrigeration Plant: Describe the proposed refrigeration system design, including refrigerant selection
and   justification,   compressor   configuration   and   redundancy,   defrost   strategy,   insulation   system,
temperature monitoring and alarm arrangements, and the testing and commissioning plan for achieving
the specified chamber temperatures.
Q3.   HSE Management: Describe how your HSE management system will be applied on this site, including
the site HSE organisation, the specific controls you will apply for work at height, lifting operations and
refrigerant handling, and your incident reporting and investigation procedure.
Q4.   Quality Assurance and Materials Testing: Describe your quality management arrangements, including
the site laboratory and testing regime for concrete and steel, factory inspection arrangements for the PEB
and refrigeration equipment, inspection and test plans, and the handover documentation you will provide.

10. CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT, PENALTIES AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
The contract shall be executed on the standard form of contract for admeasurement works adopted by the
Employer, based on the PEC standard conditions. The successful bidder shall furnish a performance security
equal to ten percent (10%) of the contract price within fourteen (14) days of the letter of acceptance, valid
until the end of the defects liability period. A mobilisation advance of up to ten percent (10%) of the contract
price may be granted against an unconditional bank guarantee, recoverable in equal deductions from interim
payment certificates. If the contractor fails to complete the works within the completion period of fourteen (14)
months, liquidated damages shall be levied at the rate of 0.1 percent (zero point one percent) of the contract
price per day of delay, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%) of the contract price, after which the Employer
may terminate the contract and complete the works at the contractor's risk and cost. The defects liability
period shall be twelve (12) months from the certified date of completion, during which the contractor shall
remedy all defects at its own expense. Retention money of five percent (5%) shall be deducted from each
interim payment certificate, one half releasable on completion and the balance on expiry of the defects liability
period. The contractor shall, before commencement, effect and maintain contractor's all risks insurance for
the full contract value plus fifteen percent (15%), third-party liability insurance of not less than PKR 25 million
per occurrence, and workmen's compensation insurance covering all site personnel, each with the Employer
named as co-insured, and shall furnish copies of the policies and premium receipts to the Engineer. The
contract shall be governed by the laws of Pakistan, and disputes shall first be referred to the Engineer for
determination and thereafter, failing amicable settlement within twenty-eight (28) days, to arbitration in
Karachi under the Arbitration Act, 1940.
